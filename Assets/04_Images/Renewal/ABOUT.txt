Renewal character samples

Three original character sprites generated using the built-in image generation tool, with native Unity transform animation clips.
References: existing Goblin Club and Witch Fire images for broad cartoon style; generated Mushroom Spearguard for consistency. Final prompts are in GenerationPrompts.json.

Open Assets/01_Scenes/RenewalCharacterPreview.unity and press Play to compare all three using the existing CharacterGallery viewer.
Prefabs: Assets/03_Prefabs/InGame/Character/Renewal. Each contains an Animator and AnimationLink on its root and a bottom-aligned SpriteRenderer on its Visual child. Animation curves only affect Visual, keeping gameplay positioning and gallery fitting independent.
Original RGBA PNG pixels and alpha are preserved. No spritesheet slicing is required.

Animations: Idle, Walk, Attack and AttackIdle (12 clips total). Use the gallery motion buttons, Pause, Speed and Flip to inspect them. These are whole-sprite squash/stretch, bounce, float and lunge animations, without independent limb/weapon poses or directional frames.

Attack is non-looping and emits Fire at its impact and AniEnd at completion, compatible with the existing AnimationLink callbacks. Other states loop. These remain visual sample prefabs without combat components or unit-table registration. Existing gameplay art is unchanged.

Editable assets: Assets/07_Animations/Character/Renewal. Rebuild with Tools > Renewal Characters > Rebuild animations; this replaces the sample prefabs and clips. Validate with Tools > Renewal Characters > Validate animations.

Validation: Unity 2022.3.47f1 built the assets and checked 96 sampled poses across all 12 clips, loop seams, gameplay-root isolation and attack callbacks in an isolated project.
