Renewal right-facing character samples

ACTIVE ART
The three active rigs use Parts/RightFacing/*_Parts.png. These are new, original cutout redraws made with the built-in image generation tool; exact prompts and reference roles are in Parts/RightFacing/GenerationPrompts.json. Earlier illustrations and front-facing atlases remain as non-destructive design history.
All active heads, side-view torsos and boot toes face RIGHT, for left-to-right movement. Flip the model root to face left. Each character keeps its original palette, equipment and identity.
Atlas slicing follows connected alpha components across cell boundaries, preserving necks and cap edges instead of clipping a strict rectangular grid. There are 16 imported sprites per atlas, 48 total.

PREVIEW
Open Assets/01_Scenes/RenewalCharacterPreview.unity and press Play, or choose Tools > Renewal Characters > Open preview.
Six motions: Idle, Walk, Attack, AttackIdle, Hurt, Death. Pause, Speed and Flip also work. Other gallery scenes retain their original four motion buttons.
The clips are in-place; the game movement system moves the unit root. The rendered walking review adds matching physical left-to-right root movement against fixed ground marks.

RIG AND WALK
Prefabs: Assets/03_Prefabs/InGame/Character/Renewal.
Compact side-view rig with nearly coincident hip joints and short sturdy limbs. The foot cycle separates planted stance from lifted recovery, solving the two leg joints to each foot position. A separate Sole/ankle joint keeps boots flat during stance and gives a small toe lift during recovery. The head counterbalances a small body bob. Equipment hands carry their load instead of making broad marching swings.
Walk cycles: spear .90s, mage .98s, shieldguard 1.04s. Stance occupies62% of the cycle over a .18-unit step. RenewalAnimationBuilder.PreviewWalkSpeed returns the matching root velocity for the review export.
Weapons follow the hand, shields follow the far hand, and the lantern hangs independently from the staff. Effects have their own root, independent of body falls.

ATTACK AND EFFECTS
Attacks have character-specific preparation, strike/cast and recovery with planted feet. Thin borderless attack streaks last briefly near the moving weapon tip; tiny sparks follow the strike. The mage releases a small tapered bolt from the sampled staff tip at Fire. No large outlined arrow, starburst sticker or unsupported mid-air target hit is used. Foot dust is subtle and appears only around contact.
Hurt uses a short recoil and mild tint. Death falls and fades. Only Idle, Walk and AttackIdle loop.
Every clip explicitly resets its animated channels. Attack emits Fire at the strike and AniEnd at completion through the existing AnimationLink interface.

BUILD, VERIFY AND EXPORT
Assets/07_Animations/Character/Renewal contains18 editable native Unity clips and3 controllers.
Tools > Renewal Characters > Rebuild animations replaces the sample rigs, controllers and clips using the active atlases.
Validate animations checks Animator state entry, clip bindings, loop closure, limb/weapon movement, VFX reset and planted-foot position/flatness over the walk cycle.
Validate preview scene checks script and prefab references plus six-motion controls.
Export motion review renders native Unity frames against fixed ground marks. Exported GIFs encode those frames without redrawing or retouching the art.
Review artifacts are in output/renewal: ArticulatedPoses.png, RightFacingWalkCycle.png and the animated right-facing walking/attack previews.

These remain visual sample prefabs: unit-table registration, combat collision and damage systems are separate from this art workflow. Original game characters and their animations are unchanged.
